# Privacy Policy — NG Browser

**Last Updated:** November 25, 2026  
**Developer:** NayaGram Platform  
**Package Name:** `org.nayagram.ngbrowser`  
**Contact:** nghuzayfa@gmail.com

## 1. Overview
NG Browser is a privacy-focused Android web browser developed by NayaGram Platform. We do **not** collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal browsing history, bookmarks, passwords, or search queries to NayaGram Platform servers.

## 2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device
All browsing data stays exclusively on your device:
- **Bookmarks, History & Download Records:** Stored locally in your device's private SQLite database (`ngbrowser.db`). You can clear them at any time inside NG Browser.
- **Private Browsing Mode:** Tabs opened in Private Mode do not save browsing history or persistent cookies.
- **NG Shields & Tracking Protection:** Enhanced Tracking Protection, Total Cookie Isolation, and HTTPS-Only mode run locally inside the standalone Mozilla GeckoView engine on your device.

## 3. Permissions Used
NG Browser only requests optional permissions when a website you visit explicitly asks for them:
- **Internet & Network State:** Required to load web pages and download files.
- **Location (Optional):** Requested only if you grant a website permission to access your location.
- **Camera & Microphone (Optional):** Requested only if you grant a website permission (e.g., video calls or voice search).

## 4. Third-Party Websites & Search Engines
When you navigate to websites or use search engines (such as DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Google, Startpage, or Bing), those external services are governed by their own privacy policies.

## 5. Data Deletion
You can delete all cookies, cache, site storage, and browsing history at any time via **NG Shields → Clear All Browsing Data**, or by uninstalling the app.
