Data Safety & Deletion Guide

Transparent disclosure of data collection, third-party sharing, and how to permanently erase local browsing records in NG Browser.

Google Play Data Safety Declaration

How to Delete Your Data

Because NG Browser does not maintain user accounts or cloud databases, all data deletion happens immediately on your device:

  1. Clear Browsing History (Partial or Full): Open NG Browser → tap the three-dot menu → History → tap Clear... and choose Clear Last 24 Hours or Clear All History. You can also tap any individual entry to remove only that link.
  2. Clear Cookies, Cache & Site Storage: Tap the NG Shields icon in the address bar → select Clear All Browsing Data.
  3. Clear Downloads List: Open Menu → Downloads → tap Clear List.
  4. Erase All App Data via Android: Go to Android Settings → Apps → NG Browser → Storage → Clear Storage, or uninstall NG Browser.
